## The Opportunity

**Problem:** Brain-computer interfaces generate the most sensitive data a human can produce — neural signals that encode thoughts, intentions, and medical diagnoses. Current BCI platforms (Neurosity, Emotiv, NeuraLink software APIs) process this data on cloud infrastructure subject to US CLOUD Act, third-party breaches, and commercial data mining.

**Solution:** L_MNECORE provides a complete sovereign BCI stack:
- Raw EEG → preprocessing → classification → AIOSS audit — entirely on-device
- SHA3-256 hash chain proving every processing step was unmodified
- HIPAA/GDPR/neurorights compliance without cloud vendor risk

**Who needs this:**
- Clinical neurology departments (EMR-integrated BCI)
- Defense/intelligence (thought-protected command interfaces)
- Neurotechnology companies requiring HIPAA compliance
- National neurorights regulators (Chile, EU under consideration)
